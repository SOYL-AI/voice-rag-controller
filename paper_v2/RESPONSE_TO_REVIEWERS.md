We thank both reviewers for careful and constructive reports. Three of the
reviewers' points changed the paper.

**A correction to the submitted Sec. V-C and Figs. 2–3.** That section and
those figures were produced from a synthetic test fixture in our repository,
not from the experiment. We discovered this while preparing the revision.
Every E3 number and figure in the revision is regenerated from the real sweep
(50 scenarios × 26 prices × 5 seeds). The analysis script now refuses to read
the fixture.

**A new framing.** Reviewer 1 showed that the null result was analytically
predictable, and Reviewer 2 asked us to keep the coupling null central and
say where joint control could help. We now present the null as the
equivalence regime of a theorem. We also derive where that regime ends.

**A new, pre-registered test.** The revision adds a pre-registered study on
larger and harder data with live ASR partials. It runs the same items under
two pipeline architectures and tests where the theory places the boundary:
joint control should pay when a dispatched retrieval is committed, and should
add nothing beyond a belief-gated decomposed prefetcher when a retrieval can
be discarded.
