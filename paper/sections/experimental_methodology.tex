\subsection{Statistical Analysis}
\label{subsec:statistical_analysis}
% TODO (Shubhankit): number of seeds (5: 11,23,37,53,71), paired bootstrap details,
% confidence intervals, primary metric (frontier dominance), treatment of multiple comparisons.
The statistical analysis was performed for five random seeds (11, 23, 37, 53, and 71) separately. Uncertainty was measured via bootstrapping for the scenarios of each operating points by means of 2,000 iterations of bootstrap procedure. A fixed random seed for bootstrapping was used to provide reproducibility. 95% bootstrap confidence intervals are provided for the following metrics: answer accuracy, p95 time to first audio (TTFA), and mean retrieval computation. The key comparison is made to check if the proposed unified controller strictly dominates the B9 baseline in terms of matched operating points. The point was strictly dominated if Ours had a higher accuracy with non-overlapping confidence interval, but had no higher p95 TTFA and retrieval computation than the corresponding confidence interval of the baseline. In order not to consider an isolated point as enough to establish dominance, it was necessary that the dominance criterion holds on at least two consecutive operating points. The final criterion of strict domination (as specified in the preregistration) consisted of at least three of five seeds having contiguous dominance region.The analysis used the preregistered dominance criterion of at least 3/5 seeds, no seed met this criterion, and therefore no multiple-comparison correction was applied.
